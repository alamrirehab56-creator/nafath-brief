## Overview
A national single sign-on system like NAFATH needs different IT fields to work together to provide secure and easy access to digital services. Software engineering is responsible for developing the system and making sure users can log in safely. Artificial intelligence can help identify unusual login attempts and detect possible threats. Mobile development focuses on protecting user information and controlling app permissions on smartphones. Networking helps keep communication between users and the system secure. Cybersecurity protects accounts from hackers and unauthorized access by using strong authentication methods. Data management ensures that users’ personal information is stored and handled safely according to data protection laws. Although each field has a different role, they all work together to make the system more secure, protect users’ privacy, and provide a smooth experience when accessing digital services.
